# What testers should try in the next build: plain text, shown at the top of
# TestFlight's What to Test. Lines starting with # are left out. Update it in
# the PR that goes into the build; keep it short. The recent changes and the
# commit are added automatically (ci_scripts/what-to-test.sh).
Thanks for testing AlgoMinutes. First, open the app (you start as a guest, no
sign-up), go to Settings, tap Enter an invite code, and enter the code from
your invitation. It adds your recording minutes.

Then please try:
1. Record a meeting of a minute or two, stop, and wait for the note. You can
   leave the app; a notification says when the note is ready.
2. Open the note: read the summary, the action items and the transcript.
3. Search for a word you said, and ask the note a question in chat.
4. Settings, then Create account, to keep your notes with Apple or Google.
5. Settings, then Go Pro. If plans show, buy one: TestFlight purchases are
   Apple's test purchases, and you're never charged. Then Restore Purchases.

Your notes are kept for the whole beta, and backed up. The public release
starts fresh: you'll be told before then, with time to export your notes.
Record only people who agreed, and nothing confidential.
