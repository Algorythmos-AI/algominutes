# Written by hand for each build that needs it (ci_scripts/what-to-test.sh).
AlgoMinutes turns recordings into summaries, decisions and action items.

- Start without an account and record a short meeting
- Agree to the Google Cloud AI sheet the first time
- Open the note: summary, transcript, Ask AI, export
- Import an audio file and scan a document
- Lock the phone after stopping and check the notification
- Sign in, sign out, and delete an account
- Go Pro and Restore Purchases (no real charge in TestFlight)
