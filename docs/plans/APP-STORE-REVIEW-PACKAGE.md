# The App Review package for 1.0

**Draft for the owner, 2026-10-29.** Checked against `origin/integration` at aa7a8e38 and the merged PRs up to
#530. Nothing was changed in App Store Connect. It builds on `docs/runbooks/app-store-review.md` (#484, #509)
and corrects it where the code disagrees ("Drift" at the end). It is the review half of R12.5 Stage 5.

Status words: **pass** = the repo shows it. **not verified** = needs a device, a console or a person.
**fail** = the repo shows it is not done.

## 1. App Review Notes

For App Store Connect › App Review Information › Notes. This version assumes call capture is out (§6). If it
stays in, swap the CALL CAPTURE paragraph for §1.1.

```
AlgoMinutes records a meeting on iPhone and turns it into a transcript and a summary.

NO ACCOUNT NEEDED
On first launch the app makes a private guest account for this device. You can record and read a summary straight away. Signing in with Apple or Google later (Settings) keeps the same notes.

FREE MINUTES
A new iPhone gets a 7-day trial with Pro's recording minutes. It needs no payment and is not a subscription. Apple DeviceCheck allows one per device. After it, or if this device has used its trial before, every account has 120 free recording minutes a month. So you can always record without buying.

TO REVIEW
1. On Home, tap "Instant Recorder" (Record and generate a smart summary).
2. A "Before you record" sheet opens. It says the audio is uploaded, then transcribed and summarised by Google Cloud's speech and AI services, and kept until you delete it. Tick "I have permission from anyone whose voice may be captured", then tap Start recording. The tick is asked for every time the sheet opens. iOS then asks for the microphone.
3. Speak for a minute and tap End. The note is usually ready within a few minutes. The app asks for notification permission after the first recording, not at launch.
4. Open the note (recent notes on Home, or Files) to read the summary, action items, key decisions and transcript. You can change the summary's template or edit it.
5. "Ask AI" (Files) answers questions about your notes.
Import Files and Scan Text on Home also make notes. A scan is read on the iPhone; only its text is saved.

AI-GENERATED CONTENT
Transcripts, summaries and Ask AI answers are generated by Google Cloud (Speech-to-Text and Gemini on Vertex AI). The "Before you record" sheet and the Privacy Policy say so. Payment card and similar numbers are removed from the text before any model sees it. Nothing is shared with other users, and nothing is used for ads or tracking.

BACKGROUND AUDIO
The audio background mode is used only to keep a recording the user started going while the screen is locked. The app never records on its own.

CALL CAPTURE
This version has no call capture and no broadcast extension. Recording uses the microphone only.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
Settings > Go Pro shows AlgoMinutes Pro, monthly and annual, auto-renewing, priced by the App Store. Restore Purchases is in Settings and on the Pro screen, and works for a guest. Redeem an offer code is in Settings. An App Store subscription is managed from Settings > Manage Subscription.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > Delete my account, type DELETE. For an account signed in with Apple, the app first revokes its Apple token (you may be asked to sign in with Apple once more). Then every note, recording and summary is deleted from our servers. A guest account can be deleted the same way.

CONTACT
Settings > Help & Support > Contact support, or the contact details in this App Review Information.
```

If the AI label (`APP-STORE-AI-LABEL.md`) ships, add to AI-GENERATED CONTENT: "Each summary and export is
marked as written by AI."

password.

## 6. Call capture at review: remove it from the 1.0 binary

**Recommendation:** take the Broadcast Upload Extension out of the 1.0 Release build and set production's
`broadcast_capture` to "off", unless proof 5 passes by 11-05. This is R12.5 Stage 5's "otherwise removed from
1.0", made concrete.

1. **Proof 5 isn't there.** A real broadcast killed mid-call, picked up and processed, has not been run. I3's
   fix (fragmented MP4, **#532**) is open; its evidence is an in-process kill test on the iPhone 11 (unit lane
   437 executed, 0 failures), and it says proof 5 is still manual.
2. **Switched off is still dormant.** The extension stays installed; a reviewer can start it from Control
   Center, and the app says capture is "turned off right now" (`AppEnvironment.swift`). That invites 2.3.1.
3. **It draws the most questions** (ReplayKit, other apps' calls, other people's consent), and mic, import and
   scan cover the core use. Call capture can ship in 1.1 with proof 5 and §1.1.

Removing it is a code change, so it restarts the 7-day soak: do it before RC1.
- `apps/ios/project.yml`: drop the `BroadcastExtension` dependency and its entries from the `AlgoMinutes`
  scheme for Release; keep the target for Staging if testers use it. Check the device lane's
  `AlgoMinutes-ReleaseUITests` scheme (#528) at the same time.
- `Info.plist`: drop the call-capture clause from `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`, and in STORE-COMPLIANCE §4.2.
- Check whether the App Group is still needed without the extension; change the manifest and docs together
  (the privacy-answers test will say).
- Production: `TF_VAR_broadcast_capture=off` through the owner's reviewed plan and apply; a saved plan resets
  Cloud Run images, so redeploy after.

If #532 lands and proof 5 passes on a device before 11-05: keep it, leave the switch on, paste §1.1.
