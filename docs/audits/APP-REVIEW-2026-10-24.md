# App Store Review readiness audit: the iOS app (2026-10-24)

> Read-only audit of `apps/ios` for plan rev 12 (`docs/plans/RELEASE.md` R12); its findings carry the rev 12
> ids (I*, S6). Audited at the local checkout b8415286 (2026-10-05); the iOS tree is unchanged at integration
> 6ce9c3f0 (the later merges are server-side). Swift paths are relative to `apps/ios/AlgoMinutes/` unless noted.
> The backend half is `PROD-READINESS-2026-10-24.md`.


## (a) Overall verdict

**Would not pass a first public App Store submission today — it cannot even produce a Release archive.** The Release configuration has empty `API_BASE_URL`/`BILLING_BASE_URL` and the project's own pre-build phase fails the build on purpose (`apps/ios/project.yml` Release block + "Backend origins are set" script), the only `GoogleService-Info.plist` on disk is staging's (`PROJECT_ID = algominutes-staging`) and nothing in `ci_scripts/ci_post_clone.sh` checks the project id, and `PAYWALL_ENABLED` is `"NO"` in Release while prod Terraform starts every new user with zero minutes (`infra/terraform/envs/prod/main.tf:143`, `trial_on_first_use = "off"`). The net effect for a reviewer on a Release build: guest start works, the first recording is refused with "Recording minutes come with an invite code" (`KickoffFailure.swift:32`), Settings offers "Enter an invite code" with a `BETA-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX` field, and there is no purchase path — a closed-beta posture that fails 2.1 and a non-IAP unlock mechanism that fails 3.1.1. The StoreKit 2 implementation itself is sound (server-verified JWS, Restore, Manage, correct 3.1.2 copy), Sign in with Apple and account deletion are implemented, privacy strings and the app's manifest are in good shape, and robustness work (interruptions, low disk, resumable background uploads) is above average. The repo's own plan already lists most of this as A1–A8 in `docs/plans/RELEASE.md:397-404`; this audit confirms those and adds a few more (extension privacy manifest, extension device family, review notes, labels/docs drift).

## (d) PASS items

- **3.1.1 mechanics (when the paywall is on):** StoreKit 2 only — `Product.products(for:)`, `product.purchase()`, `Transaction.updates` listener from init, JWS forwarded to the server (`POST verifyPurchase`) and `finish()` only after server OK; no Stripe/Checkout/web price anywhere in `apps/ios` (`StoreKitService.swift:52-56, 67, 91-110, 196-212`; `BillingService.swift`). `BILLING_BASE_URL` is used only for `/v1/purchases/verify` (`AppConfig.swift:31-33`).
- **Restore purchases:** `AppStore.sync()` + re-send of `Transaction.currentEntitlements`, reachable on the paywall and in Settings, works for anonymous guests (`StoreKitService.swift:127-146`; `PaywallView.swift:236-258`; `SettingsView.swift:254-258`).
- **Manage subscription:** `AppStore.showManageSubscriptions(in:)` with URL fallback (`StoreKitService.swift:153-168`; `PaywallView.swift:259-262`; `SettingsView.swift:261-268`); grace/billing-retry nudge (`isInGracePeriod`).
- **3.1.2 paywall copy:** price = `product.displayPrice`; period "Billed every month/year, auto-renews"; renewal terms "Your subscription auto-renews at the price shown until you cancel. Cancel anytime from Manage Subscription; access continues until the end of the current billing period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account."; links to Terms of Service and Privacy Policy (`PaywallView.swift:127, 136-148, 264-284`; `LegalLinks` → `https://algominutes.algorythmos.com/{terms,privacy}`; site pages exist: `apps/site/src/pages/{terms,privacy,support}.astro`).
- **4.8 Sign in with Apple:** offered wherever Google is, first and with equal size/prominence (`LoginView.swift:33-62`; `AccountUpgradeSheet.swift:149-177`); SHA-256 nonce; `fullName` passed to Firebase on first login (name-only-first-login handled); relay email is whatever Firebase returns, shown in Settings; guest→permanent upgrade links in place and asks before switching to an existing account (`AuthService.swift`).
- **5.1.1(v) account deletion path:** Settings → "Delete my account" → typed DELETE confirmation → Apple revocation prompt → `POST /v1/account/delete` → sign-out; server deletes Postgres first, then Firestore/Storage, then Auth, 200 only when complete; works for guests (`SettingsView.swift:137-146, 454-573`; `APIClient.swift:329-331`; `services/api/src/routes/delete-account.js`; `AppleTokenRevocation.swift:54` `revokeToken(withAuthorizationCode:)`).
- **Usage strings vs APIs:** mic (`AVAudioApplication.requestRecordPermission`, `MicPermission.swift:32`), camera (VisionKit document camera, `ScanSheet.swift:79`), photo library (`PhotosPicker`, no runtime prompt needed but string present) all have specific, accurate strings (`Info.plist` `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`/`NSCameraUsageDescription`/`NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`). No `SFSpeechRecognizer`, location, contacts, calendar, local network, or ATT use (framework import inventory: AVFoundation, ReplayKit, VisionKit/Vision, PhotosUI, PDFKit, MessageUI, MediaPlayer, MetricKit, DeviceCheck, CallKit, UserNotifications, Network, CryptoKit, Firebase*, GoogleSignIn, StoreKit).
- **App privacy manifest (app target):** `NSPrivacyTracking=false`, empty tracking domains; required-reason APIs: UserDefaults `CA92.1` + `1C8F.1` (own defaults + App Group defaults via `BroadcastHandoff`), FileTimestamp `C617.1` (`RecordingStore.swift:222,233`, `attributesOfItem` in RecorderService/ImportSheet/AudioPlayerService), DiskSpace `E174.1` (`RecorderService.swift:193-196` refuses/stops recording on low space, i.e. behaviour changes → E174.1 is the correct code, not 85F4.1). No `systemUptime`/boot-time or active-input-mode use (`ProcessInfo.thermalState` only).
- **Third-party SDK versions:** Firebase 11.15.0, GoogleSignIn 8.0.0, GTMAppAuth 4.1.1, AppAuth 1.7.6, GTMSessionFetcher 3.5.0, GoogleUtilities 8.1.3, GoogleDataTransport 10.1.1 — all at or above the versions that ship privacy manifests and signatures.
- **Background modes:** only `audio`; the recorder activates `.playAndRecord` on start and deactivates on stop (`RecorderService.swift:222-223, 330`); the player holds `.playback` only while playing and releases it (`AudioSessionCoordinator.swift`). No idle background audio session.
- **Broadcast extension conduct:** captures `audioApp` + `audioMic` only, video explicitly ignored; 4 h cap; disk check; iOS owns the picker/red indicator; a capture started from Control Center is not uploaded until the user confirms permission in-app, and the acknowledgement covers one capture (`SampleHandler.swift:221-222`; `BroadcastPickerView.swift`; `ConsentGate.swift`; `RootView.swift:100-113`; `AppEnvironment.swift:478-534`). Mic string discloses "your side of a call in another app". UI copy says "Record a call (Zoom, Teams, Meet)" and the runbook says "your side and the other side of a call" — the other party's audio is captured, so the legal exposure in `docs/CONSENT.md` (NSW all-party, no opinion yet) applies; the UI makes no legality claim.
- **Consent gate:** mandatory per-session checkbox before every recording, enforced in `RecorderService.start()` (`RecorderFlow.swift`; `RecorderService.swift:207-209`).
- **DEBUG gating:** admin cost card, dev guest sign-in and the "Skip sign-in (dev)" button are under `#if DEBUG` (`SettingsView.swift:98-100, 575-648`; `LoginView.swift:71-82`; `AuthService.swift:152-169`). Staging is a release-type config, so none of it ships to testers either. No "coming soon" screens; server-switched features are hidden, not dead buttons. No `TODO` text in UI strings.
- **4.5.4 push:** permission requested only after the first recording starts, after a one-per-launch pre-prompt explaining the purpose; APNs registration only then; only `note_ready`/`note_failed` pushes exist server-side; token deleted on sign-out (`AppEnvironment.swift:195-203`; `RecordingNotifier.swift:23-57`; `services/notifier/src/handler.js:18,39`; `PushTokenRegistrar.swift`).
- **Force update:** any 426 → `APIError.updateRequired` → full-screen `UpdateRequiredView` with friendly copy and an Update button; server floor from the contract (`APIClient.swift:131-139, 686`; `RootView.swift:52-54`; `UpdateRequiredView.swift`; `packages/contracts/src/version.ts`; `services/api/src/client-version.js`). Only the URL is wrong (G1).
- **2.3.10:** no Android/Play/other-platform references in UI strings (only code comments). YouTube import deliberately not offered on iOS (`ImportSheet.swift:5-7`).
- **Encryption:** `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption=false` is correct — only HTTPS and CryptoKit SHA-256 hashing (`AuthService.swift` nonce, `CrashReporting.swift:19`).
- **Build identity:** bundle id `com.algorythmos.algominutes` (+ `.BroadcastExtension`) in every config; `MARKETING_VERSION 1.0.0`, `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION 14` (Xcode Cloud stamps `CI_BUILD_NUMBER`); app `TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = 1`, `LSRequiresIPhoneOS`, portrait only, no iPad keys needed; `aps-environment` is `development` in the entitlements file, which Xcode replaces with `production` for TestFlight/App Store signing (verify in the archive's entitlements); deployment target iOS 17.
- **Crash/robustness scan:** no `try!`/`fatalError`; the few force unwraps are guarded (`HomeView.swift:57`, `ChatView.swift:245`, `PDFExporter.swift:42`, `SendNotetakerSheet.swift:58-60`). Interruptions: `.began/.ended` handled, resume attempted even without `.shouldResume`, watchdog with `CXCallObserver` so a long call pauses rather than stops, route loss, media-services reset, thermal logging (`RecorderService.swift:676-762, 433-509`). Low disk: 250 MB at start, 50 MB floor every 30 s, 300 MB in the extension. Uploads: background `URLSession`, GCS resumable sessions resumed from the server's byte offset, stall/ceiling watch, join-not-duplicate for tasks surviving a kill, local copy kept until the kickoff is settled; a 4 h recording is ~115 MB against a 500 MB server cap (`BackgroundUploadService.swift`; `UploadStallPolicy.swift`; `AppEnvironment.swift:315-331, 420-438`).
- **Ratings prompt:** `SKStoreReviewController.requestReview(in:)` once per version, only after a summary renders (`AppReviewController.swift`; `NoteDetailView.swift:328`).
- **Tests (not run):** `AlgoMinutesTests` — 47 XCTest files, 376 `func test…` methods (167 in `AlgoMinutesTests.swift`); `AlgoMinutesUITests` — 1 file, 1 launch-smoke test. No Swift Testing `@Test`.
